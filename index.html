How to Play

Every turn is a question. Four choices, one correct answer, one countdown clock.

Answer correctly → your Pokémon strikes. Damage scales with type matchups (just like the real games), your streak, and the question's difficulty.

Answer wrong or run out of time → your opponent strikes back, and your combo streak resets to zero.

Campaign mode is a 10-stage gauntlet ending in a Champion battle. Endless mode throws increasingly tough opponents at you for a high score.

Progress and stats are saved on this device automatically.
